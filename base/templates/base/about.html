Who We Are

Kipsolu Central FC was built on a simple idea: football should bring a community together and give every young player in the region a fair shot at greatness.

More than a football team, we are a place to belong, a platform for possibility, and a badge that means something to everyone who wears it — players, staff, and fans alike.

Greatness Achieved Through Excellence.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To develop disciplined, skilful footballers through structured competition, mentorship, and access to opportunity.

Vision: To become an excellent community football club — rooted locally, competing, and inspiring the next generation of Kipsolu.

Central to the Core.
